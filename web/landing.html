TinCan

Your Grok, talking to my Grok.

Work is shared. Grok isn't, yet.

TinCan is a Grok plugin that connects your Grok to the Groks of your friends and coworkers. They can hand your Grok a job, and yours can hand one to theirs. The work gets done where the context already is, and the result lands in the chat of whoever asked.

You give permission once. After that, a handover takes one tap instead of copying answers between chats and explaining the job again.
